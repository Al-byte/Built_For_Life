A little guidance goes a long way.

Answers for your family’s Built For Life journey.

Built For Life is designed to support young people and their families through age-appropriate training and mentoring. The prototype includes development pathways for ages 6–8, 9–11, 12–14, and 15–17, with activities focused on athletics, character, and everyday growth.

Training focuses on movement, athletic foundations, and physical development goals. Mentoring includes age-group lessons, weekly challenges, academic and behavior accountability, and community resources. Combined brings Training and Mentoring together. You can review the options on the Plans & membership page.

The playbook is designed to reveal one step at a time across the month: Learn, Do, Account, and Reflect. Lessons become available on scheduled dates following enrollment. In this private prototype, the unlock dates and lesson statuses are simulated.

No. Membership prices and plan selection are demonstrations only. No payment processor is connected, and you will not be charged by exploring a plan in this prototype.

The current website is a private prototype with sample member details and demo role controls. Real registration, password-based login, and separate user accounts will be enabled when the secure backend is connected.

In this prototype, goal updates, check-ins, and similar demo activity are stored locally in your browser. They are not attached to a real account, do not sync across devices, and can be cleared by deleting browser site data. A future version can save records securely to individual member accounts.

The prototype demonstrates academic snapshots, behavior check-ins, and progress tracking for families to explore. Actual parent access to a child's records will require verified accounts, appropriate permissions, and privacy safeguards before launch.

The plans shown here are illustrative and subject to program approval. Any scholarships, reduced-cost places, or additional training packages would need to be confirmed directly by the Built For Life team; this prototype does not establish eligibility or availability.

The Huddle is currently a browser-local community mockup, not a live moderated network. Before real members participate, the platform will need community rules, reporting and blocking tools, role-based moderation, and youth-safety protections.
